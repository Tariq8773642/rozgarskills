How to Make a Professional CV in Pakistan: A Beginner's Guide

A CV is often the first thing an employer sees when you apply for a job. A good CV does not need complicated design. It should be clear, accurate and focused on the job you want.

1. Start with your basic information

At the top of your CV, write your full name and appropriate contact details. Include a phone number and a professional email address. Avoid unnecessary personal information.

2. Write a short career objective

For a beginner, a two or three sentence objective can explain what type of opportunity you are looking for and what skills you can offer.

3. Add your education

List your highest or most relevant qualifications first. Include the name of the institution, qualification and year where useful. Do not claim a qualification that you have not completed.

4. Describe your work experience

If you have experience, mention your job title, organization and approximate period of employment. Add two or three bullet points describing your actual responsibilities and achievements.

5. Include useful skills

Choose skills that are relevant to the position. Examples may include MS Word, Excel, typing, communication, customer service, record keeping or other skills you genuinely have.

6. Keep the CV easy to read

  • Use clear headings.
  • Keep formatting consistent.
  • Use short bullet points instead of very long paragraphs.
  • Check spelling and phone numbers carefully.
  • Usually keep a beginner CV to one or two pages.

7. Common CV mistakes

  • Using false information
  • Adding an unprofessional email address
  • Writing the same generic CV for every job
  • Including irrelevant information
  • Leaving spelling and grammar errors
  • Using too many colors, fonts or decorative graphics
Tip: Before sending your CV, read the job advertisement again and make sure your CV clearly shows the education, experience and skills that are relevant to that position.

Simple CV structure

Name & Contact
Career Objective
Education
Work Experience
Skills
Certifications or Courses (if applicable)
References (if requested)

Conclusion

A professional CV is mainly about clarity and honesty. Keep it simple, highlight relevant experience and skills, and proofread it before submitting your application.
